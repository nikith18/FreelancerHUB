# FreelancerHub UI requirements

- Give every card a clearly visible surface in both light and dark themes. Use a defined border, a subtle inner edge highlight, and layered outer depth; keep the result readable and restrained.
- Apply the shared surface and shadow tokens in `client/src/index.css` to new card components. Keep existing cards consistent with those tokens when changing the UI.
- When visual preview is available, inspect card depth in both themes and at the relevant responsive widths.
